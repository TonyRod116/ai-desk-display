    How to read the dashboard

    Game Boy Green, Game Boy Pocket, Pixel Retro, Amber Terminal and Orbit show weekly quota remaining and the time until the weekly reset. Expand a card to see other limits, such as the current session. Other dashboard themes show quota used and the busiest limit. Official readings disappear when a refresh fails, they become more than three minutes old, or their reset time passes. Unknown readings appear as —.

    Hearts count saved resets; they are never calculated from the quota bar. When automatic checks are enabled, balances come from the official account readers. Otherwise, confirm them manually in the official Usage settings. An unknown balance appears as ?.
