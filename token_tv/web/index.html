How to read the dashboard

Each card shows weekly quota remaining and the time until the weekly reset. Claude's current 5-hour window, which is what stops work first, sits right under it. When Codex's weekly allowance is exhausted, its card shows extra credits if the official reading provides them; the empty bar still represents weekly quota. Official readings disappear when a refresh fails, when they are more than eight minutes old, or when their reset time passes. Between three and eight minutes the number stays, marked with a small dot. Unknown readings appear as —.

Hearts count saved resets, which are separate from extra credits and are never calculated from the quota bar. When automatic checks are enabled, balances come from the official account readers. Otherwise, confirm them manually in the official Usage settings. An unknown balance appears as three faint hearts and a small dot. The chart, when there is one, is only the recent pace carried forward: it changes as your use does, and it stays blank when there is too little history or the usage is flat.
