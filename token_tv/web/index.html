How to read the dashboard

Each appearance shows weekly quota remaining and the time until the weekly reset. Expand a card to see other limits, such as the current session. When Codex's weekly allowance is exhausted, its card shows extra credits if the official reading provides them; the empty bar still represents weekly quota. Official readings disappear when a refresh fails, they become more than three minutes old, or their reset time passes. Unknown readings appear as — or ?.

Hearts count saved resets, which are separate from extra credits and are never calculated from the quota bar. When automatic checks are enabled, balances come from the official account readers. Otherwise, confirm them manually in the official Usage settings. An unknown balance appears as ?.
